# Business Overview

RagWorks is a document intelligence platform for small businesses. It ingests
policies, terms, and marketing material and answers questions with citations.

## Revenue share model

The revenue share model is 70% to the host and 30% to the platform for
marketplace transactions. Enterprise plans use a flat monthly fee instead of a
revenue share.

## Available cities

The service is available in Pune, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Hyderabad, and Chennai.
Expansion to Delhi is planned next quarter.
